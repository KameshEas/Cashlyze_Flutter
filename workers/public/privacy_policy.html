Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 20, 2026

Welcome to Cashlyze. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Important: Cashlyze is a personal finance management app that stores all your financial data locally on your device and/or in your personal cloud storage (Google Drive). We do not have access to or control over your financial data.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and authentication credentials (via Firebase Authentication).
  • Financial Data: You voluntarily input:
    • Transaction records (amount, category, date, notes)
    • Budget allocations and thresholds
    • Custom categories
    • EMI/Loan plan details
  • Preferences: Currency selection, date format, alert settings, and biometric preferences.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Analytics Data: We collect anonymized usage statistics through Firebase Analytics to improve app functionality. This includes:
    • App features used
    • Screen navigation patterns
    • Performance metrics
  • Device Information: Basic device information (OS type, app version) for troubleshooting purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Cashlyze service
  • Enable account creation and authentication
  • Store and sync your financial data across devices
  • Send budget alerts and notifications (as per your preferences)
  • Improve app functionality and user experience
  • Respond to your support requests

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Local Storage

Your financial data is stored locally on your device using encrypted storage (flutter_secure_storage). This data includes:

  • Transactions
  • Budgets
  • Categories
  • EMI/Loan plans
  • User preferences

3.2 Cloud Storage

When you use our backup features, your data is:

  • Stored in your personal Google Drive account
  • Encrypted before upload using secure encryption standards
  • Only accessible by you (we do not have access to your backup files)

3.3 Firebase Services

We use the following Firebase services:

  • Firebase Authentication: Manages user accounts and authentication
  • Firebase Analytics: Collects anonymized usage analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Reports app crashes for debugging
  • Firebase Firestore: Optional cloud sync (user-controlled)
Note: Firebase services may collect certain device and usage data as per Google's Privacy Policy. We encourage you to review Google's Firebase Privacy Policy.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Cashlyze does NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share information in the following circumstances:

  • With Firebase/Google: As necessary to provide our services (see Firebase Privacy Policy)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or in response to valid legal requests
  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly authorize sharing

5. Your Rights and Choices

5.1 Data Access and Portability

You can export your data at any time through the Settings menu:

  • Export to JSON file
  • Copy to clipboard
  • Backup to Google Drive

5.2 Data Deletion

You can delete your data in the following ways:

  • Use the "Clear All Data" option in Settings
  • Delete your account from the Settings menu
  • Request data deletion by contacting us

5.3 Privacy Controls

You can manage the following in Settings:

  • Enable/disable budget alerts
  • Configure alert threshold percentage
  • Set alert frequency (daily/weekly/monthly)
  • Enable/disable biometric authentication

6. Children's Privacy

Cashlyze is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

7. Third-Party Services

Cashlyze uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase: Authentication, Analytics, Crash Reporting
  • Google Drive: User-controlled data backup
  • Google Sign-In: Authentication option

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy on this page
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date
  • Notifying you through the app (for significant changes)

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
